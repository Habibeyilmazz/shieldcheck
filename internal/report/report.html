What it looks at

  • Security headers: CSP quality, HSTS, framing, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy, COOP and CORP
  • TLS: certificate expiry and chain, protocol versions
  • Cookie flags: Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite
  • HTTP to HTTPS redirects and mixed content
  • Email DNS records: SPF and DMARC
  • security.txt

What it does to the site

A handful of ordinary GET requests, a few TLS handshakes and two DNS lookups. Nothing is fuzzed, guessed or brute-forced, and every finding comes with a fix you can paste into nginx, Apache, Caddy, Next.js, Vercel or Netlify.
